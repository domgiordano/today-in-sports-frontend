How it works

  1. 1

    The record, not the rumour

    Every game ever played is scanned — 235,512 of them so far — straight from official archives. No articles, no summaries, no hearsay.

  2. 2

    Rules decide what mattered

    A no-hitter is hits === 0. A title is clinched when the lead can no longer be caught. Significance is computed, never guessed.

  3. 3

    A human signs off

    Every question is reviewed before it reaches you, with a link back to the exact record it came from.

It gets harder as it goes back

The five questions walk backwards through time. Question one is from the past year. Question five reaches into the deep archive — but it is built to be worked out, not just remembered.
